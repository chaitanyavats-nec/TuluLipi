Tulu-Tigalari Keyboard

Type Tulu with a simple Latin-phonetic system and see it in Tulu script as you type.

  • Long Press: hold a key such as n, t or s, slide to a variant and release.
  • Shift Layer: tap ⇧ for the diacritic letters, or slide across letters to combine sounds.
  • Tap a suggestion to complete the word.
  • A physical keyboard works too.
